Classic Anzac Bisuits

A chewy, golden bisuit with a history dating back to World War I.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup plain flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup desiccated coconut
  • 125 g butter
  • 2 tablesppons golden syrup

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 160°C
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl
  3. Melt the butter and golden syrup together
  4. Stir the wet mix into the dry mix.
  5. Roll into balls and bake

Baking guide

Texture Time Temperature
Chewy 12 minutes 160°C
Crunchy 18 minutes 160°C
